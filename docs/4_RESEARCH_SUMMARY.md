## Cross-Bank Structural Patterns

### 1. Monthly Caps Are Universal and Restrictive
Every cashback card verified has monthly caps, typically AED 150–500 per category. **No cashback card in the verified set exceeds AED 1,000/month in a single category.** This means:
- High spenders (AED 15K+/month) will hit caps early and need portfolio diversification
- Portfolio of 3–4 cards often beats any single card by 2–3x in annual value

### 2. Points vs. Cashback: Cashback Is More Transparent
Points-based cards (FAB Rewards, ENBD Skywards, Citi ThankYou, SC 360°, DIB Wala'a) all require knowing the redemption rate to calculate effective return. Cashback cards (FAB Cashback, Mashreq Cashback, Liv Cashback, HSBC Cash+, DIB Consumer Cashback) provide transparent AED value directly.

### 4. UAE-Only Cards: A Significant Hidden Limitation
Several cards earn **zero** on international transactions, which is not prominently advertised:
- **ENBD LuLu 247:** Cashback on UAE spend only
- **Mashreq Cashback / Noon:** UAE spend only
- **DIB Consumer Cashback:** UAE transactions only (explicitly stated)
- **Liv Cashback / Cashback+:** UAE only (implied)
These cards are effectively useless for travelers or online purchases from foreign merchants.

### 5. Category Exclusions Are Inconsistent Across Banks
There is no standard across UAE banks for which categories earn:
- **Utilities:** FAB du earns 15% at du merchants specifically; DIB (4%), ADIB (4%), Wio (0.5%) earn on general utility bills; most cashback cards earn 0%
- **Government fees:** Earns on DIB (4% on Salik/NOL only), Wio (0.5%) — typically 0% elsewhere
- **Rent:** Typically 0% or general rate; RAKBank explicitly 0%; Wio 0.5%
- **Insurance:** Varies — Citi excludes insurance; most cards treat it as general spend

### 6. Minimum Spend Thresholds Are Common
Several cards require a minimum monthly spend to earn at the bonus rate:
- FAB Travel Card: AED 5,000 previous month
- FAB Cashback: AED 3,000 previous month
- FAB du Credit Card: AED 2,500 previous month (for 15% du rate; below this, du spend earns only 0.15% low-interchange)
- FAB Rewards Active: AED 3,000/month for free ADV+ gym access (no impact on earn rates)
- DIB Consumer Cashback Platinum: AED 4,000/month
- DIB Consumer Cashback Reward: AED 3,000/month
- ENBD LuLu 247 Platinum: AED 3,000/month (for 7% rate)
Users who don't meet these thresholds may earn at a reduced or zero rate.

### 7. Islamic/Shariah Cards: Similar Earning, Key Exclusions
Verified Islamic cards (FAB Cashback Islamic, ADIB, DIB) follow similar earning structures to conventional counterparts but with:
- MCC-level blocking of haram merchants (alcohol, gambling, tobacco) — not visible to end user, handled by issuer
- DIB covered cards are Salam-based (purchase finance structure), not conventional credit
- No meaningful difference in effective_return_pct for Shariah-compliant cardholders in most categories

### 9. Closed-Ecosystem "Mega-Bonus" Cards (Developer/Retail Loyalty Co-Brands)
ENBD has launched three co-branded card families tied to UAE master-developer/retail loyalty ecosystems, all following the same structural pattern:
- **U by Emaar** (Family/Signature/Infinite): base UPoints rate of 1.0%/1.25%/1.5%, with a **5x bonus (5.0%/6.25%/7.5%)** at Emaar malls (Dubai Mall, Dubai Marina Mall, Dubai Hills Mall), Emaar Hospitality hotels/restaurants (Address, Rove, Vida), and Emaar Entertainment venues.
- **Darna by Aldar** (Select/Signature/Infinite): base Darna Points rate of 0.75%/1.0%/1.5%, with an even larger **~8x bonus (6.25%/7.5%/10.0%)** at Aldar-ecosystem destinations in Abu Dhabi (Yas Mall, Al Qana, Aldar-affiliated hotels/beach clubs, Miral theme parks via partnership, golf, attractions).
- **SHARE Visa by Majid Al Futtaim** (Platinum/Signature/Infinite/Private — all 4 entered): base SHARE Points rate of 0.75%/1.0%/1.5%/2.0%, with a **~5.3x bonus (4%/6%/8%/10%)** at SHARE-ecosystem destinations (Mall of the Emirates, City Centres, Carrefour, Vox Cinemas, Ski Dubai, Snow Abu Dhabi, Magic Planet, and a long list of MAF-owned retail brands). Private additionally adds a suite of "Visa Private"/Elite Banking-exclusive lifestyle benefits not present on the other 3 tiers (DUBZ, YQ Meet & Assist, BookingBash Pro Ultimate, Agoda/booking.com/IHG/gettransfer.com discounts, OneVasco Concierge, Supper Club Dining Membership, Bliss Club Beach Discounts, Padel Courts, Harrod's Gold Membership) — a NEW pattern distinct from the ecosystem-bonus structure: invitation-tier cards bundle premium lifestyle partnerships on top of the scaled earn rates.

**Common structural traits:**
- All three reduce groceries/insurance to **~25% of the base rate**, and fuel/utilities/government/education/rent to **~10% of the base rate** — a "general spend is penalized" design that only pays off if the cardholder genuinely shops within the ecosystem.
- All three carry **account-wide combined monthly caps** (not per-category) that scale with tier, capping effective annual value even for ecosystem-loyal spenders.
- All three are most valuable for users who live/work/shop heavily within the issuing entity's footprint (Downtown Dubai/Dubai Hills for Emaar; Yas Island/Saadiyat for Aldar; Mall of the Emirates/City Centres for MAF/SHARE) — and comparatively weak general-spend cards otherwise (0.75%–2.0% base vs. 1%+ on many cashback cards).
- **U by Emaar and Darna** explicitly **exclude rent/real-estate and education spend from the mega-bonus**, even at properties/schools owned by the same developer — you can't earn the bonus by paying your Emaar/Aldar rent or school fees. **SHARE** doesn't need an explicit carve-out here since rent/education already sit in the lowest-rate bucket and were never eligible for the ecosystem bonus to begin with.
- **⚠️ SHARE-specific quirk — "EU Spends (incl. UK)" maps to the GROCERY-REDUCED rate, not the general rate.** Darna and U by Emaar both treat EU/UK spend at the General Domestic/International rate (same as other international spend). SHARE's T&C explicitly carves out a separate "EU Spends (including UK)" row with the SAME value as the grocery/fast-food/insurance-reduced bucket — meaning EU/UK spend earns noticeably LESS on SHARE cards than on Darna/U by Emaar cards at the same tier. Always check whether "EU Spends" gets its own row before assuming it matches the general international rate.
- **⚠️ 4th-tier discovery pattern differs:** Darna's 4th tier (Infinite Privilege) and U by Emaar's tiers all had identical OR proportionally-named rates that didn't require new entries beyond the 3 requested. SHARE's 4th tier (Private) has genuinely HIGHER per-category percentages than Infinite (10%/2%/0.5%/0.2% vs 8%/1.5%/0.375%/0.15%) — a reminder that "4th tier discovered" does not always mean "identical rates, document only"; check the actual numbers in the rate table every time. SHARE Private was entered as card #86 once the user requested it (2026-06-16).
- **⚠️ card_tier enum gaps for invitation-only tiers:** "Visa Private" (above Visa Infinite) is not in the card_tier enum (classic/gold/infinite/platinum/signature/standard/titanium/world/world_elite). Mapped to `world_elite` as the only enum value representing "above infinite". Also, min_salary_aed was NOT published for SHARE Private in any source (invitation/relationship-based tier) — stored as `null` and flagged for human review, rather than guessed.
**Learning:** When verifying developer/retail-affiliated co-brand cards, expect a "low general rate + large bonus at owned properties/partners" pattern — check for an ecosystem partner directory in a dedicated benefits PDF or T&C footnote, and check EVERY row of the rate table (including "EU Spends") rather than assuming it mirrors a previously-verified sibling card family.

- **DIB:** T&C explicitly states rates are "published on DIB website" — product pages used as verified source
- **ADCB:** Supplemental benefits T&C covers perks only; earn rates confirmed from ADCB product page PDFs and Schedule of Fees (403 on adcb.com was bypassed via direct PDF downloads)
- **ADIB:** Earn rates found in a separate "Comparison of Card Offering" PDF, not the main T&C

### Forex Markup Corrections Made During Verification
Several cards in the initial scrape had wrong forex markups:
- HSBC cards: 2.99% → **2.0%** (corrected from hsbc.ae)
- DIB cards: 2.99% → **3.70%** (corrected from KFS PDF)
- ENBD: some cards corrected to 1.99%
